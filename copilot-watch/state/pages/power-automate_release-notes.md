## Release Plans are retiring
Release Plans will no longer be published starting in September 2026. New Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and Dataverse capabilities will be published to the AI at Work roadmap beginning in September 2026. Existing release plans will remain available for historical reference until further notice. View resources below to visit the AI at Work roadmap and read the blog announcing these changes.
